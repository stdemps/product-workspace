> **Use this instead of a PRD when you need a decision, not a spec.** A PRD tells a
> team what to build once the decision is made. A one-pager gets the decision. If
> someone can say yes or no to this in two minutes, it is doing its job.

> Keep it to one page. If it grows past that, the idea is not clear yet — or it
> needs a PRD instead.
